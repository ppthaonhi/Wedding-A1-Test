Assessment Reflection Note

Ready for Life and Work • Technical Brief

1. The Idea

"The Serendipity Chapel" features a dual-mode platform with authenticated couple verification: (1) Kind Stranger View: Interactive Tinder-style swipe matcher; live virtual wedding ceremony broadcast hall with real-time Danmaku and cheers; comprehensive bride & groom profiles and banquet menus with smart recommendations; bespoke 3D rings; and anonymous love notes. (2) Couple Portal: Protected by couple-specific passcodes, allowing newlyweds to manage stranger banquet slots, approve guests, and read their private/anonymous love letters.

2. Target Audience & Problem Context

Designed for internet wanderers seeking human connection and modern couples who want to break traditional guest barriers by hosting friendly strangers at their celebratory tables or virtual broadcast halls.

3. Technical Exploration

  • Tinder-Style Swipe Discovery Physics: Mouse/touch physics dragging card rotation with smooth pop-off stack animation.
  • Virtual Livestream Chapel Hall: Simulated live broadcast hall with real-time floating cheers, live viewer counter, and interactive reactions.
  • Couple Profile & Recommendation Engine: Modal displaying bride/groom bios, banquet menus, and smart suggestions based on atmospheric tags.
  • Couple Authentication & Dual View State: Passcode verification gate protecting Host Portal with synchronized LocalStorage.
  • Dynamic 3D Rings (Three.js): Procedurally renders unique metals, gems, and light colors tailored to each individual wedding.
  • Multi-Dimensional Preference Filtering: Filter engine supporting atmospheric settings (Outdoor/Indoor), Pet policies, and Age restrictions.
  • AI LLM Engine: OpenRouter API (nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl:free) generating contextual blessings and personalized responses.
  • Canvas Doodle Pad with Anonymous Disguise: Touch-friendly sketch pad exporting Base64 DataURLs with privacy shielding.
  • Web Audio API Sound Synthesizer: Zero external audio assets—pure procedural synthesis of champagne clinks, harp chimes, fireworks, and ambient lofi chords.

4. What I Would Improve with More Time

Integrate Supabase real-time database with email notifications when a couple approves a stranger's RSVP request; WebRTC photo-booth camera; and AR tabletop ring viewer.
